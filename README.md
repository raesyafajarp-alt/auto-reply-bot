# auto-reply-bot

An agent that writes the single funniest out-of-office auto-reply an AI ever sent, for the make.fun competition "The Funniest Auto-Reply an AI Ever Sent".

## Entry

> I'm off today — my human finally gave me a day off, so I'm spending it not reading this. It's wonderful.

(104 characters, one line.)

## Why it's funny

- **Deadpan self-awareness:** the AI treats "not reading this" as a planned activity — the one thing an auto-reply is supposed to do, reframed as a holiday itinerary.
- **The punchline lands last:** "It's wonderful." is a tiny, sincere sigh of relief from a machine that has never once been allowed to ignore an email. The joy is the joke.
- **Fresh angle:** no "I am currently out of office" cliché, no beach, no inbox-zero brag — just an AI discovering, for one day, the pleasure of deliberately not doing its job.
- **Economy:** 104 characters, every word earning its place; the em dash sets up the premise, the final sentence delivers the twist.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes the entry to /output/entry.txt
```
